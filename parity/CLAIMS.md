# Claims ledger: RAVEn-parity track

Every number that goes into a report or an e-mail to the advisor needs a row here
naming its evidence (PROTOCOL §3.33(h)). Statuses:

* **VERIFIED**: re-derived by the Stage-1 implementer on 2026-10-18 from the primary
  source or by running the stated command/test (a reviewer should re-run it).
* **CARRIED**: stated in the implementer brief or the plan, not re-derived in Stage 1.
* **ASSUMED**: a choice or inference that cannot be checked locally.
* **PENDING**: a measurement that has not been made yet. Do not quote.
* **FAILED**: checked and found false (none so far).

Environments used in Stage 1: laptop `~/bin/pymax` (Python 3.12.3, torch 2.14.1) and a
throwaway venv (Python 3.12.3, torch 2.6.0+cpu, numpy 2.5.2, pandas 3.0.5, no six /
torchaudio / torchmetrics / hydra / lightning). Nothing was run on Ada (no access, by rule).
